Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how True Taste handles information related to this recipe website.

True Taste operates this website as an informational platform for food recipes and related content. This Privacy Policy explains, in a clear and straightforward manner, how information may be collected, used, and safeguarded when you visit or interact with this site.

Information We May Collect

When you visit the website, certain information may be collected automatically or when you voluntarily provide it, such as:

  • Contact details that you choose to submit, such as your name and email address, for example when using a contact form or subscribing to a newsletter.
  • Technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, which may be logged by servers or analytics tools.
  • Interaction information, such as the time and date of visits, click patterns, and general usage data.

Use of Collected Information

Information collected through this website may be used for purposes including:

  • Responding to inquiries or messages you submit.
  • Improving the content, layout, and overall experience of the site.
  • Monitoring site performance, security, and technical functionality.
  • Analyzing aggregate trends to understand how visitors engage with recipe content.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to support core functionality, remember preferences, or understand how visitors use the site. Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser.

You can typically manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. If you choose to disable cookies, some portions of the site may not function as intended, but core informational content should remain accessible.

Analytics and Third-Party Services

True Taste may use third-party analytics services to help evaluate website usage. These services may collect information such as IP addresses, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the site. The information is generally used in aggregated form to understand traffic patterns and improve the website.

Third-party providers have their own privacy policies and may process information in accordance with their terms. Reasonable efforts are made to work with providers that handle information in a responsible and privacy-conscious manner.

California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

For visitors from California, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) may provide certain rights regarding personal information, including the right to request access to, or deletion of, certain information, subject to legal limitations. At this time, this website does not sell personal information.

California residents who have questions about how the CCPA may apply to this site can contact True Taste using the email address listed below.

General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

For visitors from the European Union or other regions where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, True Taste aims to handle personal data in a manner consistent with the principles of transparency and fairness.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights such as access, correction, or deletion of certain personal data, and the ability to object to or restrict specific types of processing, subject to legal requirements.

Third-Party Links

The website may reference or link to third-party sites, services, or content. These external sites are not controlled by True Taste, and their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Visitors are encouraged to review the privacy notices of any third-party sites they visit.

Data Security

Reasonable steps are taken to maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards intended to protect information collected through the website from unauthorized access or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be fully secure.

Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. True Taste does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this site, please use the contact email below so the matter can be reviewed.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in the website, applicable laws, or privacy practices. When changes are made, the “Last Updated” date may be revised. Continued use of the site after updates are posted indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
